Candidate Overview

Industrial electrical experience with mine-site exposure

Vexon Recruiting is confidentially representing a fourth-year electrical apprentice with direct experience supporting electrical work at remote mine sites and within demanding heavy-industrial environments.

His background includes motor control centre installations, cable terminations, equipment testing, fault diagnosis and work on energized systems up to 25 kV under established lockout, grounding and electrical-safety procedures.

He has supported planned mine-site shutdowns alongside Red Seal electricians and has additional project exposure across an LNG facility, hospitals, commercial construction and industrial sites.

He is seeking a permanent or ongoing industrial electrical opportunity offering FIFO, camp or rotational work. Relocation would be considered where the overall position and package provide the right long-term fit.
