Immediate Civil Construction Candidate Interest

Concrete finishers, paver operators, pipelayers and top men

Northern Alberta • Immediate availability required

Candidate interest is being accepted now. Final employment and logistics details are being confirmed. Requirements may change quickly, and recruitment may close once the necessary personnel have been identified.

The Opportunity

Time-sensitive Northern Alberta requirements

Vexon Recruiting is identifying experienced civil-construction candidates for anticipated immediate requirements in Northern Alberta.

Current requirements include concrete finishers, asphalt paver operators, water and sewer utility pipelayers, and top men. Project work is expected to continue until approximately October 15, subject to weather.

Employer and project details will be discussed directly with qualified candidates before any résumé or identifying information is submitted.

Core Requirements

What candidates need

  • Immediate or near-immediate availability
  • Relevant role-specific experience
  • Valid driver’s licence
  • Clean driver’s abstract

Current Requirements

Roles being prioritized

CONCRETE

Concrete Finishers

Minimum one year of relevant experience. Include the types of concrete finishing you have performed and your earliest availability.

PAVING

Asphalt Paver Operators

Minimum three years of relevant experience. Summarize your asphalt-paving experience and the projects and equipment you have worked with.

UNDERGROUND

Pipelayers

Minimum three years of water and sewer underground-utility experience. Oil and gas transmission pipeline experience alone does not meet this requirement.

TOP MAN

Top Men

Minimum three years of relevant underground-utility experience. Must communicate clearly from above the trench with pipelayers and equipment operators.

Position Details

Project and compensation details

Work is expected to start as soon as possible and continue until approximately October 15, subject to weather. Compensation will be discussed with qualified candidates.

Exact locations, schedules and travel or accommodation arrangements will be discussed as the remaining details are confirmed.

Providing complete, current information will help Vexon assess your potential fit quickly.
